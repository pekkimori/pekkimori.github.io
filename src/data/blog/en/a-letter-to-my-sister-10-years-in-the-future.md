hey!  
well, you know I'm not a very formal person, I'm kinda crazy and stuff.
so don't expect some pretty little letter hahaha.
by the time you receive this letter, you'll be the same age I am now, 20 years old.
which is kinda crazy for me to think about, because in my head, you're still that baby who used to throw up milk on me while watching cartoons.

I've never been one to think much about my past, maybe that's why I forget things so easily, and because of that, it's hard for me to tell whether people close to me were worth my love or not.
and I've taken some pretty hard hits recently because of it.
but that taught me to love the people who really matter.
you probably don't even know it or remember it, but you helped me realize that.

a few months ago, it was during some random conversation, on some random day, I don't even remember the context, I just remember Mom saying to me: "Clara respects you a lot. You're basically her father figure.", and you butted in from across the room: "How do you know?!", and Mom replied: "Everybody knows, you can tell just by the way you listen to him."
and the thing is, I hadn't realized it.

that's when I stopped to think, our dad was never very present, and to me that was already normal, but I'd never really thought about what that meant for you.

there were months when it was just me, Mom, and you at home. it was kinda like taking turns, I took care of you when she had to work, she took care of you when I went to school.
it was in those moments that went by in the background that so many things I have and am today were probably built; like why family is something so important to me.

I always wanted to take care of the family, or rather, I always had to.
even if they were the source of the most devastating events that happened in my life.
and I went through so much that you can't even imagine.
there were moments I felt no hope where we lived.
the reason I decided to leave the country was exactly that.

I could list all my traumas here for you, but that’s not my goal for this letter, you don’t need to take the burden from my past.
even though I didn't ask for or create any of that, as unfair as it was, I was born into these problems, forced to accept everything and take responsibility for whatever happened.
the thing is that it didn't matter whether it was unfair, what mattered was whether things were getting better.

I tried to carry everyone in my arms, even people who only wanted to hurt me.
even so, I decided not to hate anyone. I decided to learn how to love.
and that was the only reason why it improved.
the life you are living right now, is so far from the best, yet gladly so far from the hell it was a few years ago.

up until then, all of that had slowly built a mindset that made me accept whatever shit people threw at me.

it took me a long time to learn to distinguish love from responsibility and respect.
in parts, what I mean is that you can love people without putting up with their shit.
I also mean that you can respect people and make commitments to them without having any emotional bond with them.

knowing that, choose carefully the people you decide to truly love.
there's a difference between love (wanting what's best for people, and seeing them as imperfect people just like you), and love. it's not something you can explain, if you haven't realized it yet, you'll learn that with time (hint: you can feel it in your gut).

both you and I have very sensitive hearts. (and I know that, I know you better than our own father does) so make sure you give yours to people who will know how to take care of it with tenderness.

that's not contempt, it's not disrespect, it's not wrong.
on the contrary, it's what will fill your life with people who will allow you to be yourself.
love those who know how to love.

it was after that conversation that I realized the responsibility the universe had left for me, and I'm already used to accepting whatever it throws at my head.

the thing I wish for the most is that you don't have to go through the hardships I went through. I want you to be free to choose your beliefs, your future, your connections.

at the moment I'm writing this letter, you're still a child.
and a child doesn't need to carry all that weight just to be able to live.
so, in part, I confess that I carry it and hide it from you.

but when you're reading this letter, you'll already be an adult.
and you probably still won't even know why I do what I do.
but I want you to know that at your age, I did what I did, in part, for you.

love you,  
from your brother Rafa.
