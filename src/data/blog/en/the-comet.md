Everything is always moving  
and with time we always change.  
I'm small within this chaos,  
trying to make sense of strange.

Every wonder needs a witness  
as beauty withers when it's sealed.  
For what survives isn't the moment,  
it's the memory made real.

When a hundred sextillion lights glow,  
They are too many for one frame.  
Yet they pull me through the cosmos,  
while some will never know my name.

Distant worlds became departures  
and distant orbits drew me near.  
Every planet left an echo  
for every memory settled here.

I wish I shone just like the sun,  
instead I burn just like a comet:  
leaving light where there was none,  
never meant to overcome it.

Not enough to light the heavens,  
not enough to make them mine;  
only enough that where I wander,  
one more streak of light may shine.

For the fragments leave their scars,  
as they cross another sky,  
fading into shooting stars,  
small lights before they die.

Maybe what I leave burns bright  
long after I disappear.  
Maybe someone sees that light  
in the trail that brought me here.

Maybe hope lives in the traces  
I decided to leave behind,  
in the echoes of old spaces  
from the selves I left in time.
